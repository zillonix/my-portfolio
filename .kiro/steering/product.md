## Messaging & Positioning

- Lead with recent, verifiable work. Every project links to a real GitHub repo where one exists.
- Pair each project with outcomes and the reasoning behind technical choices, not just a tech list.
- Surface academic research (Uber breach ethics, Shopee UX redesign) as evidence of analytical and communication skills.

## Tone of Voice
- Professional, grounded, and confident without hype. Show, don't tell.
- Concrete and specific: real stacks, real repos, measurable highlights.
- Concise copy. Recruiters skim; let structure and hierarchy carry the message.
- Avoid superlatives ("best", "world-class") and filler. Prefer verifiable claims.

## Success Signals
- A recruiter can determine fit and download the CV within ~15 seconds.
- A team lead can open any featured project and understand the architecture and the candidate's role.
- The site loads fast, works on mobile, and is fully keyboard and screen-reader accessible.
